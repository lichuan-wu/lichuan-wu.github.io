## Overview

This bachelor/master course (1ME418, 5 hp) covers the fundamental principles of atmospheric radiation, including shortwave and longwave radiative transfer, the greenhouse effect, and applications in remote sensing and climate science.

## Topics

- Blackbody radiation and Planck's law
- Solar radiation and the atmospheric energy balance
- Longwave radiation and the greenhouse effect
- Radiative transfer equation
- Remote sensing of the atmosphere
- Climate feedbacks involving radiation

## Format

- Lectures
- Problem-solving sessions
- Written examination

## Target audience

Bachelor and Master students in Meteorology, Physics, and related disciplines.
