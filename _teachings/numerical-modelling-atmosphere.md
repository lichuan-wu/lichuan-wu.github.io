## Overview

This master course (1ME406, 10 hp) introduces the numerical methods used in atmospheric modelling, from simple one-dimensional models to state-of-the-art Earth System Models. Students gain hands-on experience running and evaluating atmospheric models.

## Topics

- Finite-difference and spectral discretisation methods
- Numerical stability and accuracy
- Parameterisation of sub-grid processes
- Numerical weather prediction: data assimilation and forecast verification
- Regional and global climate models
- Coupled Earth System Models: atmosphere, ocean, waves, sea ice
- Model evaluation and bias correction

## Format

- Lectures and seminars
- Computer labs (model runs and post-processing in Python)
- Group project: design and evaluate a modelling experiment
- Written report and oral presentation

## Prerequisites

Introductory courses in atmospheric dynamics, calculus, and basic programming (Python preferred).
