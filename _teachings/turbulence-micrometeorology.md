## Overview

This master course (1ME405, 15 hp) provides a rigorous introduction to turbulence in the atmospheric boundary layer and its role in weather and climate. Particular attention is given to the marine environment and air–sea interaction.

## Topics

- The atmospheric boundary layer: structure and scaling
- Turbulence statistics and spectral analysis
- Monin–Obukhov similarity theory
- Surface energy balance and turbulent fluxes
- Measurement techniques: sonic anemometers, eddy covariance, etc.
- Air–sea interaction: momentum, heat, and gas exchange
- Ocean surface waves and the marine boundary layer

## Format

- Lectures and seminars
- Data analysis exercises
- Field/lab component
- Written examination and project report

## Prerequisites

Introductory courses in fluid mechanics, atmospheric dynamics, and mathematics (calculus, statistics).
