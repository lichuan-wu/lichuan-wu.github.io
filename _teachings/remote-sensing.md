## Overview

This bachelor course (1ME410, 10 hp) provides a thorough introduction to remote sensing of the atmosphere and ocean surface, covering both passive and active satellite instruments used in operational meteorology and climate research.

## Topics

- Electromagnetic spectrum and radiometry
- Satellite orbits and sensor types
- Passive microwave and infrared remote sensing
- Active remote sensing: radar and lidar
- Applications: sea ice, sea surface temperature, precipitation, atmospheric sounding
- Case studies in weather analysis and climate monitoring

## Format

- Lectures
- Laboratory sessions with satellite data analysis
- Project work
- Written examination

## Prerequisites

Basic courses in meteorology, mathematics, and physics.
