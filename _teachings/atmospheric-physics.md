## Overview

This master course (1ME401, 15 hp) covers the fundamental physical processes governing the atmosphere, including thermodynamic processes, cloud formation and microphysics, atmospheric radiation, and large-scale energy balance.

## Topics

- Atmospheric thermodynamics: parcel theory, stability, convection
- Cloud microphysics: nucleation, growth, precipitation
- Atmospheric radiation: shortwave and longwave processes
- Radiative–convective equilibrium
- The general circulation and energy balance
- Applications to weather and climate

## Format

- Lectures
- Problem sessions
- Laboratory exercises
- Written examination

## Prerequisites

Basic courses in meteorology, calculus, and classical physics.
