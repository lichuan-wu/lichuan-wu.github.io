A recurring thread in our work is to build coupled modelling systems in which the atmosphere, ocean waves, the ocean and sea ice exchange information consistently, and to use them where the coupling matters most.

- **Coupled modelling.** We developed fully coupled atmosphere–wave–ocean model systems for the Baltic Sea and North Sea, and evaluate how wave coupling changes simulated storms, coastal upwelling, water level and the regional climate.
- **Offshore wind.** Offshore wind farms modify the wind, the waves and the air–sea fluxes around them. We study wave blocking and wave–structure interaction at turbine foundations, wind farm wakes on ocean surface waves, and how wave–air coupling can improve offshore wind energy forecasts.
- **Maritime transport and emissions.** With colleagues at Wuhan University of Technology we work on ship routing in heavy weather, remote monitoring of ship emissions, and the environmental trade-offs of green shipping.
