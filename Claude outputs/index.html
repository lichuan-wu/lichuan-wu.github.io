Lichuan Wu

Associate Professor (Senior Lecturer) in Meteorology · Department of Earth Sciences, Uppsala University

I am a meteorologist and physical oceanographer at Uppsala University working on air–sea–ice interaction and the role of ocean surface waves in the Earth system. My research spans from micro-scale turbulent fluxes at the air–sea–ice interface to global-scale impacts on climate, with a particular focus on the marginal ice zone and the Arctic.

My work integrates observational campaigns, remote sensing, and coupled numerical models (atmosphere–wave–ocean–ice) to understand how momentum, heat, and mass are exchanged across the air–sea–ice boundary and how these exchanges feed back into weather and climate systems, including polar lows and atmospheric rivers.

I hold a Docent (Associate Professorship) in Meteorology from Uppsala University (2021). Before my current position, I was an Assistant Professor at Uppsala (2019–2022) and held postdoctoral positions at SMHI, the Norwegian Meteorological Institute, and Johns Hopkins University. I have received grants from the Swedish Research Council, Formas, Carl Tryggers Stiftelse, and the Horizon Europe programme, totalling more than 10M SEK as PI.

I am currently on an international sabbatical at the University of Reading, UK (2025).
